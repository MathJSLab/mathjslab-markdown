# Sums and vectors: components and dot product

**Extensions:** `mathjslab`, `mathjax`.

## Key ideas

Vectors use two components. Addition combines corresponding components; the dot
product adds component products. The executable block needs a MathJSLab
executor and host execution authorization.

```mathjslab
u = [2, 1];
v = [1, 3];
u + v
sum(u .* v)
```

$$u\cdot v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2.$$

## Practice

Find u+v and u·v before running. Do the results have the same type?

## Answer with explanation

u+v=[3,4] is a vector. u·v=2×1+1×3=5 is a scalar.
