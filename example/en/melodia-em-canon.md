# Canon: delayed voice entry

**Extensions:** `abc`.

## Key ideas

Both voices share the same note sequence; the second enters one measure later.
A rest fills that voice’s opening measure. Delayed imitation is the idea of a
canon.

```abc
X:1
T:Short canon
M:4/4
L:1/4
K:C
%%score (1 2)
V:1 clef=treble
C D E F | G A G2 | z4 |]
V:2 clef=treble
z4 | C D E F | G A G2 |]
```

## Practice

How many measures does each voice last? Why should we not add the voice
durations?

## Answer with explanation

Each voice occupies three measures including rests. Voices occur
simultaneously, so the piece lasts three measures.
