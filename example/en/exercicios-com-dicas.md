# Exercises with hints: successive percentages

**Extensions:** `didactic`, `mathjax`.

## Key ideas

A hint guides without revealing the whole answer. Open it after trying to model
both changes; the solution can be consulted separately.

:::: exercise Increase and discount

A value of 100 increases by 20% and then decreases by 20%. Does it return to
100?

::: hint Multipliers

Use factors 1.2 and 0.8. The second percentage applies to the already increased
value.

:::

::::

::: solution Answer with explanation

$100\times1.2\times0.8=96$.

:::

## Practice

What is the overall percentage change? Explain why the two rates do not cancel.

## Answer with explanation

The overall decrease is 4%. The two percentages have different bases.
