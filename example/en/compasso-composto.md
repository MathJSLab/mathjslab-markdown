# Compound meter in 6/8

**Extensions:** `abc`, `mathjax`.

## Key ideas

In 6/8, six eighth notes can form two groups of three. The base length `L:1/8`
makes durations explicit; each group occupies a dotted quarter note.

```abc
X:1
T:Two groups of three
M:6/8
L:1/8
Q:3/8=60
K:G
GAB cBA | G3 D3 | G6 |]
```

## Practice

How many eighth notes fit in each measure? How many main groups are there?

## Answer with explanation

Six eighth notes, arranged in two groups of three: $2\times3=6$.
