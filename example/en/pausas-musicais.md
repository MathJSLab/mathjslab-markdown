# Musical rests: silence has duration

**Objective:** keep the beat through rests and check measure durations.
**Extensions:** ABC, MathJax.

## Key ideas

A rest indicates an interval without a note in that voice, but musical time
continues. In the original exercise below, 4/4 contains four quarter-note
beats. `L:1/4` assigns a quarter note to an unmodified note.

```abc
X:1
T:Rests and beat
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=80
K:C
C D z2 | E2 z G | c4 |]
```

In ABC, `z` is a rest and `z2` lasts two beats in this setting. The first
measure has C, D and a two-beat rest: $1+1+2=4$. In the second, E lasts two
beats, the rest one and G one. The final high C, `c4`, lasts four beats.

Count aloud or mark the beat with your hand without filling the rest with a
note. Rests help organize the phrase. If the application offers audio, use it
to check duration without replacing reading.

## Practice

1. How many beats of rest occur in the exercise?
2. Does a rest’s duration depend on the preceding pitch?

## Answers with explanations

1. Three: two in the first measure and one in the second.
2. No; it is determined by the rhythmic value.
