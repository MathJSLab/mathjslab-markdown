# Ammonia: pyramidal geometry

**Extensions:** `molecule-3d`, `smiles`, `mathjax`.

## Key ideas

Ammonia $\ce{NH3}$ has three N–H bonds and a lone pair. XYZ coordinates are an
illustrative trigonal-pyramidal model, not a measurement. SMILES alone does not
determine the spatial geometry.

```smiles
N
```

```xyz
4
Illustrative ammonia model
N 0.000 0.000 0.300
H 0.940 0.000 -0.100
H -0.470 0.814 -0.100
H -0.470 -0.814 -0.100
```

## Practice

How many atoms are in the model? Is the whole molecule planar?

## Answer with explanation

Four atoms. No: nitrogen lies outside the plane of the three hydrogens.
