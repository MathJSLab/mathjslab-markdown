# Ode to Joy: melody, beat and phrases

**Objective:** read a melody in C major and recognize repetition and variation.
**Extension:** ABC. **Prerequisite:** musical note names.

## Before playing

This is an educational arrangement of Beethoven's melody, transposed to C
major. The time signature is 4/4: each measure contains four quarter-note
beats. Start slowly, marking the beat before playing the pitches.

```abc
X:1
T:Ode to Joy - educational arrangement
C:Ludwig van Beethoven
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=88
K:C
E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | E3/2 D/2 D2 |
E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | D3/2 C/2 C2 |
D D E C | D E/2 F/2 E C | D E/2 F/2 E D | C D G,2 |
E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | D3/2 C/2 C2 |]
```

## Reading the musical code

In ABC, `C D E F G A B` correspond to do, re, mi, fa, sol, la and ti. `L:1/4`
sets the quarter note as the default duration. `D2` lasts two beats; `E3/2`
lasts one and a half beats; `D/2` lasts half a beat. The comma in `G,` puts
that G one octave below `G`.

The second phrase repeats most of the first but changes the ending. Returning
to C creates a sense of tonal rest. If the application offers audio, start
playback using the score control.

## Practice

1. Add up the durations in the fourth measure.
2. Identify the measures that begin the first and second phrases.
3. Sing the melody using note names before increasing the tempo.

## Answers with explanations

1. $1.5+0.5+2=4$ beats.
2. Measures 1 and 5 begin E, E, F, G.
3. This activity lets you check pitches separately from instrumental technique.
