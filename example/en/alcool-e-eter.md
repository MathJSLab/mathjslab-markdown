# Alcohol and ether: same formula, different bonds

**Extensions:** `smiles`, `mathjax`.

## Key ideas

Ethanol and dimethyl ether share formula $\ce{C2H6O}$ but differ in
connectivity. In `CCO`, terminal oxygen receives an implicit hydrogen; in
`COC`, it connects the two carbons.

```smiles
CCO
```

```smiles
COC
```

## Practice

Which drawing contains the O–H group? Is the molecular formula enough to
distinguish the substances?

## Answer with explanation

CCO represents the alcohol with O–H. The molecular formula is identical;
connectivity is needed to distinguish them.
