# Digital Electronics

An undergraduate-level reference on digital electronics, covering the
mathematical foundations, electronic implementation, analysis, synthesis,
verification, and applications of digital systems.

## Languages

- **Português brasileiro:** [Eletrônica Digital — sumário](pt/README.md) —
  versão em elaboração.
- **English:** translation planned after the Portuguese edition is finalized
  (`en/`).
- **Español:** traducción prevista después de concluir la edición en portugués
  (`es/`).

The language directories will share the same English, lowercase,
hyphen-separated chapter filenames. Each chapter will be an independent
Markdown file. At this stage, only the Portuguese table of contents is
published; links to chapters will be enabled as they are written.
